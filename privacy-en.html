Privacy Policy

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data collected via mrartemyfit.com is Artem Antonenko, working as a personal and medical trainer in Bielsko-Biała, Poland. Data protection contact: antonenkoartem199@gmail.com or Telegram @mr_artemy.

2. What data is collected

Through the contact form on this site, only the data you provide yourself is collected:

  • name,
  • contact details (Telegram and/or phone number),
  • optionally — information about your health, injuries or limitations, if you choose to describe them in your message (this constitutes a special category of personal data under the GDPR).

This data is sent directly to the administrator's private chat in Telegram (via the official Telegram Bot API) and is not stored on the website's server.

3. Purpose and legal basis for processing

Data is processed in order to respond to your enquiry, prepare a training or nutrition offer and arrange cooperation — on the basis of your voluntary consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and in the case of health data — on the basis of explicit consent to process special category data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

4. Retention period

Data is retained in the administrator's Telegram chat for the time necessary to handle the enquiry and any subsequent cooperation, no longer than necessary, unless longer retention is required by separate legal provisions.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies for statistical purposes (e.g. Google Analytics), exclusively after you give consent via the banner displayed on the site. You may change your decision at any time by clearing your browser data for this site. The site does not use cookies for advertising or profiling purposes.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data,
  • rectify your data,
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict processing,
  • object to processing,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal,
  • lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, Poland).

7. Data recipients

Data is transferred solely to Telegram Messenger Inc. as the operator of the messenger through which the administrator receives messages. Data is not sold or shared with any other third parties for marketing purposes.
