Principles

WHAT THE SYSTEM IS MADE OF

  • 01

    Individuality

    Every program is built from scratch — around your goal, fitness level, health, lifestyle and the time you can commit. I don't fit the person to a ready-made scheme; I build the scheme around the person. That's why two clients with the same goal almost never get the same program.

  • 02

    Rationality

    I don't complicate the process where it doesn't bring a meaningful extra result. I don't make training complicated just to look like "professional work" — my job is to find the simplest, most effective path to the goal. If two solutions give a comparable result, we choose the one that's easier to follow and fit into your life.

  • 03

    Training

    Designing the training process starts with the goal and the starting data. I choose exercises based on biomechanics, your build and any limitations, set the volume and intensity that drive progress while still allowing recovery, and plan load progression so results keep coming steadily. Technique gets special attention: quality of movement matters more than the weight on the bar.

  • 04

    Nutrition

    The nutrition format is chosen individually: some people do best with precise calorie and macro tracking, some with a ready-made meal plan, others with a looser system without counting. The more precise the approach, the more predictable the result; a looser format is easier, but the result is gentler. We choose what you can realistically stick to long term.

  • 05

    Recovery

    Results are built not only during training but between sessions too. I take sleep, overall load, stress, daily activity and other factors that affect recovery into account, and adjust the program when they change.

  • 06

    Monitoring

    A program isn't a document written once. I keep assessing results — how you feel, progress in training and changes in your body — and make adjustments. If something stops working, we change it rather than wait.

SCIENCE, PRACTICE AND THE WHOLE PICTURE

MY APPROACH

Science + practice + an individual approach.

I don't chase every new trend. My work is based on fundamental principles of physiology, anatomy, biomechanics and training methodology, backed by science and years of practice. I also follow the professional NSCA standards in my work.

I don't complicate training just to look like "professional work." My job is to find the simplest and most effective path to your goal.

Every program is built from scratch — around your goal, fitness level, health condition, lifestyle and the time you're willing to commit.

COMPREHENSIVE WORK

I don't just work with training. Depending on your goal, the system may include training, nutrition, recovery, the psychological side, and sports supplements.

When needed, I also take into account test results, medical records, injuries, musculoskeletal features and other factors that can affect the outcome. I use all of this within the limits of my professional competence — treatment and rehabilitation remain the domain of doctors.
