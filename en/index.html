What you get

YOU DON'T PAY FOR A WORKOUT.
YOU PAY FOR A SYSTEM.

I analyse your goal, starting point, lifestyle, fitness level and health specifics, and then build an individual strategy.

Training, nutrition and recovery become parts of one system rather than a set of separate recommendations.

All the complex work — analysis, calculations, exercise selection and adjustments — stays with me. You get a simple, clear plan you can actually follow in everyday life.

Individuality

I don't fit you to a ready-made program — I build the program around you. Every plan starts from scratch.

Rationality

I don't add complexity where it doesn't pay off. If two options work equally well, we pick the one that fits your life more easily.

Science + practice

Decisions are based on physiology, anatomy, biomechanics and experience with 100+ clients. Every element of the program has a clear purpose.

A comprehensive approach

Training, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle are one system. With health limitations, the process is adapted to specialists' recommendations.

TRAINING THAT TAKES
YOUR HEALTH INTO ACCOUNT

Medical limitations don't always mean giving up training. More often they mean the load needs to be adapted correctly.

I work with people who need a more careful and individual approach to building their training process.

In short

Spine · joints · cardiovascular system · carbohydrate metabolism · thyroid · getting back in shape after surgery and injuries, and other conditions.

MORE ABOUT MEDICAL FITNESS
Important

Medical Fitness doesn't replace treatment or rehabilitation. The work is done within a trainer's competence and only when a medical specialist has cleared you for physical activity.

About me

ABOUT ME

As a child and teenager I was overweight, and I was bullied at school because of it. So I decided to change my body: I started with boxing, then moved to the gym, began watching my nutrition and taking control of my training. I reached my goals — and realised how much I love this field.

Today nothing gives me more satisfaction than taking someone from point A to point B and changing their life. But in a way that they don't repeat my mistakes: back then I tried plenty of ineffective and needlessly complicated methods. That's why, with me, everything is done properly and rationally from day one.

Over 3+ years I've worked with 100+ clients and coached more than 4,000 personal sessions.

I keep deepening my knowledge of anatomy, physiology, biomechanics, nutrition and training design — and apply it directly in my work with clients.

EDUCATION

  • Global Fit Education — EQF 4 · personal trainer and nutritionist
  • Grand.Fit Academy — ISO standard certificate · personal trainer and nutritionist
  • Additional training in adapting workouts to health conditions
  • In my work I follow NSCA professional standards and fundamental science

FAQ

Do I need experience?
No. I work with beginners as well as experienced athletes.
Do I have to count calories?
Not always. The nutrition format is chosen to suit your goal, individual needs and what's convenient for you.
Can I train with health issues?
In many cases, yes. The training process is adapted to your specific situation and your medical specialists' recommendations.
Which format is right for me?
It depends on your goal, starting point, fitness level and how much support you want. We can find the best format during a free consultation.
Where do the sessions take place?
At FC24 (formerly CityFit), Bielsko-Biała.
How much does it cost?
The price depends on the format. You'll find prices above on this page, and full details in the Services section.
