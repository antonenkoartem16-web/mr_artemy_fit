> We are looking for a supplier of standardized plant extracts in powder form for our first production batch.
> I found your company online. Could you please give me a WhatsApp number or an email of someone from the sales team?
> I'd like to send our full list with specifications and quantities, so you can check what you can supply. It's a long list, so it's easier in writing.
> Thank you!

Якщо питають, що саме: "Six extracts: rhodiola, ginseng, saffron, beetroot, melissa and spearmint. Small quantities for a first batch."

Якщо кажуть, що мало: "Do you have a partner or distributor for small quantities?"

Якщо просять написати польською: "Could you tell me the email, and I'll send it in Polish."

Корисні фрази:
- "Could you repeat the number, please?" — Чи не могли б ви повторити номер?
- "Could you spell the email, please?" — Продиктуйте по буквах, будь ласка.
- "Who should I address the message to?" — Кому адресувати повідомлення?
- "Thank you, I'll send it today." — Дякую, відправлю сьогодні.
